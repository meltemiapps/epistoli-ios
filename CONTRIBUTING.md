# Contributing

Thank you for helping. Epistoli is licensed GPL-3.0-only.

## Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)

Every commit must be signed off, certifying the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/):

```sh
git commit -s
```

This adds a `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>` line.

## Contributor Licence Agreement (CLA)

Pull requests from outside contributors are not accepted yet. They will be accepted once a CLA is published in `CLA.md`. The CLA will be based on the Apache Individual Contributor License Agreement v2.2, adapted for Meltemi Apps. Issues and bug reports are welcome now.

## Rules

- No analytics, crash reporting, ad SDKs or tracking of any kind.
- Never commit secrets, keys or signing material.
- Do not hard-code brand strings; run `scripts/brand_check.sh`.
